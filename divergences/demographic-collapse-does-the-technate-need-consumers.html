The engine treats demographics as background data in individual entries but never questions the structural assumption: does the centralized power structure need a large consumer base to function? Two contradictory readings: (1) demographic collapse ACCELERATES the centralized power structure — algorithmic labor replacement becomes necessity, not choice; smaller population is more controllable; pension system collapse forces dependence on state/corporate infrastructure. (2) Demographic collapse BREAKS the centralized power structure — the system that extracts wealth from the public requires a consumer base generating revenue; real estate, retail, healthcare all depend on population growth; tax base erosion makes infrastructure maintenance impossible. The engine must decide: is the centralized power structure building for 10 billion people or 4 billion? The answer changes every prediction. Falsification: track whether centralized power structure-aligned policy promotes or discourages fertility. If pronatalist policies emerge from the same nodes building the Genesis architecture, the engine's extraction model needs revision.
