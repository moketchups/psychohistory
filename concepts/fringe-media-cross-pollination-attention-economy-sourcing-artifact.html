What is Fringe-Media Cross-Pollination (Attention-Economy Sourcing Artifact)?

Plain Read: A documented pattern where apparent 'independent-source triangulation' is actually the artifact of a small interconnected fringe-media ecosystem where content circulates across ~20 podcasts + YouTube channels.

Plain read: A documented pattern where apparent 'independent-source triangulation' is actually the artifact of a small interconnected fringe-media ecosystem where content circulates across ~20 podcasts + YouTube channels. When three apparently-different sources (e.g. Nick Redfern *Final Events* 2010 on Collins Elite + Diana Pasulka *American Cosmic* 2019 on Tyler D + Ammon Hillman on Greek pharmakeia + Chuck Swindoll Jr. on Khazarian Mafia) all circulate through Danny Jones Podcast + Jesse Michels *American Alchemy* + Julian Dorey + TrueAnon — that isn't three independent sources agreeing on one underlying reality. It's one media substrate cross-pollinating its content. Methodology: when the engine sees apparent independent-source convergence, check whether the sources actually circulate in the same attention-economy cluster. Cross-pollination is not triangulation. Claims of E-Hypothesis triangulation across sources inside this ecosystem do NOT independently strengthen E-Hypothesis probability.
