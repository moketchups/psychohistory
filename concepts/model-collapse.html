Psychohistory Engine Concept — Plain English Explanation

What is Model Collapse?

When AI trains on AI output, it loses the tails of its distribution — nuance, edge cases, minority data — and converges on a distorted mean. The same thing happens to civilizations that lose contact with ground truth. The same thing happens to financial markets when BlackRock's Aladdin algorithm’s $35 trillion creates uniform outputs.
