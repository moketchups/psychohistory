Plain read: The specific 4th-century moment when the Church + Roman legal architecture shifted the Greek word *pharmakeia* (originally meaning drug administration / botanical medicine) into the Latin *maleficium* (meaning harmful magic + idolatry). This linguistic + legal shift forced pharmacology-practicing folk healers + pagan-mystery adherents + localized spiritual practitioners into a shared negative space — the newly-invented juridical category of heresy. Anchor events: Council of Elvira Canon 6 (~305 CE) + Council of Laodicea Canon 36 (~363 CE). Both explicitly tie punishment to 'unauthorized manipulation of spiritual forces / idolatry' NOT to pharmacology-as-such. This is engine-integrated as documented structural bottleneck within the Occult Lineage / Antinomian Chain scorecard — it did NOT create the Antinomian substrate (which predated) but it DID create the juridical-category mechanism that forced the substrate into an organized underground stream.
