What is Sacramental-Information-Network?

In plain terms

Plain Read: The Catholic Church's pan-continental psychological-intelligence architecture institutionalized by Fourth Lateran Council 1215 (Canon 21 *Omnis utriusque sexus*).

Plain read: The Catholic Church's pan-continental psychological-intelligence architecture institutionalized by Fourth Lateran Council 1215 (Canon 21 *Omnis utriusque sexus*). Mandatory annual auricular confession to a local priest, for every Catholic from peasant to monarch. Operational effect: every European community had a local node receiving detailed psychological + operational information from every parishioner, flowing through the parish → diocesan → curial structure. Protected by the seal of confession, enforced by the Apostolic Penitentiary. The most effective information-gathering mechanism in pre-modern human history — centuries before state bureaucratic apparatuses developed comparable capabilities. Currently under pressure from modern privacy laws + mandatory-reporting statutes attempting to legally breach the confessional seal.
