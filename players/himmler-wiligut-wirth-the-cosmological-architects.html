Personnel Core of the Inner Echelon

Heinrich Himmler (Reichsführer-SS): institutional architect who engineered the SS as dual structure — public military-bureaucratic / inner ritual-cosmological. Holocaust machinery + Kammler Sonderstab + Ahnenerbe running simultaneously under one roof.
Karl Maria Wiligut (SS-Brigadeführer, alias Weisthor): Himmler's private seer 1933-1939. Introduced Irminist cosmology and proprietary runic system — synthesis of Guido von List's Armanen runes + the Ilm rune + the Wendehorn. Operationalized into SS symbology (Totenkopf honor ring, Wewelsburg geometry). The man who drew the Black Sun mosaic.
Herman Wirth: Ahnenerbe co-founder 1935, first president. Nordic-Atlantean ideological framework anchored in the disputed Oera Linda book. Academically marginalized and pushed out by 1938. The Nordic-Atlantean seed he planted was transmuted decades later by Landig and Serrano into the Hyperborean-survival mythos that operationalizes the egregore's spatial placeholder.
