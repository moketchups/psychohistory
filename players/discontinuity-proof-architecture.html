Why Secret Societies Survive What States Cannot

A state relies on geography, constitutions, and public compliance. When the capital falls, the state dies. A secret lodge relies on oath-bound loyalty, compartmentalization, and hidden membership. When the state falls, the lodge executes its survival protocol.

Documented examples: Freemasonry survived the French Revolution. The Jesuit Order survived its suppression (1773-1814).
